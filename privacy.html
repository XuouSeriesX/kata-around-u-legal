Privacy Policy

KATA Around U — Content Publisher · Last updated: July 30, 2026

KATA Around U — Content Publisher ("the App") is a private automation tool operated solely by the creator of the "KATA Around U" content channel, used only to publish that creator's own content to that creator's own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Data the App processes

The App only handles the OAuth access token and refresh token issued by TikTok to its owner's own TikTok account, used solely to authorize publishing videos through TikTok's Content Posting API. The App does not collect, request, process, or store any data belonging to any TikTok user other than its owner.

What the App does not do

  • It does not collect personal data from TikTok viewers, followers, or any third party.
  • It does not read, analyze, or store TikTok users' content, comments, or messages.
  • It does not use TikTok data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond publishing the owner's own videos.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer any data to any third party.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored locally on infrastructure controlled by the App's owner and are used only by the automated publishing process described above.

Revocation

The App's access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account's own security/connected-apps settings, which immediately invalidates the App's ability to publish further content.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
